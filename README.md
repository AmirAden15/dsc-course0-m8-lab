# Aviation Accident Analysis

This project is about injury severity and aircraft destruction in aviation accident records from 1983 onwards, based only on professionally built airplanes.

# The Findings

- Recommended small airplane makes are BOMBARDIER and AVIAT AIRCRAFT INC.
- Recommended large-airplane makes are MCDONNELL DOUGLAS and BOMBARDIER INC.
- Model recommendations are CESSNA 180C and PIPER PA-18A 150 for smaller airplanes and BOEING 737-800 and BOEING 777 for larger airplanes. These are based on low average fatal/serious injury fractions.
- IMC accidents had higher injury fractions and destruction rates than VMC accidents.
- Instructional flights had lower injury fractions and destruction rates than Personal and Business flights.
